# How to launch.

1. download the latest ZIP file
2. drag the ZIP into your desktop
3. extract the ZIP
4. double click the main folder
5. you should see some files and folders
6. double click the index.html
7. and it should automatically open your browser
8. now ur officially in!
9. click on the screen to activate the game, just once! (it doesnt start at first cause ur not in)
10. Now look around with your mouse
11. move with "WASD"
12. interact with "E"
13. walk to the vault door to open the bunker and unlock the AI.
